About Rahul Shinde

Systems Architect, Privacy Specialist & AI Engineering Manager based in Mercer Island, WA.

Career Journey

My engineering foundation was built at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur, where I earned my B.Tech in Computer Science and developed a deep appreciation for core algorithms, system design, and rigorous problem-solving.

Over the past 20+ years, my career has evolved across four distinct eras of technology:

  1. Systems & Graphics Engineering (Adobe & Primus/ATG): Developed core plugins for Adobe InDesign 1.0 and Illustrator 9.0 in C++, followed by Flex/Java UI frameworks for ATG AssetManager.
  2. Startup Founding & Leadership (Edulabs): Founded Edulabs, architecting multi-tenant Java backends (`Qloud Server`) and tablet assessment apps, driving 100% annual revenue growth over 3 years and earning the 2014 NASSCOM Social Innovation Award.
  3. Enterprise Big Data & Cloud Scale (Oracle, Integris & OneTrust): Designed big data analytics on Apache Flink and Hadoop YARN at Oracle; led the Data Discovery Platform at Integris and OneTrust, scaling privacy compliance infrastructure across 1,200+ global enterprises and co-inventing US Patent WO2022159901A1.
  4. AI-Augmented Privacy Leadership (RelyanceAI & Walgreens Boots Alliance): Embedded AI agent workflows (Claude Code, BMAD method, Ollama APIs) into production microservices, building HIPAA-compliant DSR audit trails and achieving 60x throughput increases.
"Good engineering leadership combines architectural clarity with operational predictability—building systems that are fault-tolerant under pressure and teams that deliver with confidence."

Engineering Philosophy

1. Privacy as an Architectural Property

Privacy is not a compliance checkbox added before launch. It must be built directly into data schemas, field-level encryption, and zero-trust storage boundaries.

2. Clarity Over Complexity

Distributed systems fail at unhandled boundary conditions. Simple interface contracts, explicit data ownership, and clean ADRs outlast clever custom frameworks.

3. Human-in-the-Loop AI Rigor

AI coding assistants are powerful multipliers, but they require strict automated test suites, static analysis, and human architectural oversight to prevent technical debt.

4. Operational Predictability

Engineering excellence is reflected in steady release cadences (shifting from monthly to weekly), low P0/P1 incident counts, and high team morale.
